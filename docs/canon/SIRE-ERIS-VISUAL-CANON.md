## Studio requirements

- SIRE and ERIS must be shown using their actual canonical supplied artifacts wherever those images are presented.
- Do not substitute generated portraits, text-only reconstructions, unrelated search results, or approximate lookalikes.
- Their faces, facial proportions, complexion, eyes, defining features, hairstyles, braids, and identity-bearing symbols are immutable.
- SIRE retains her red/gold/green visual identity. ERIS retains her violet/silver/lunar visual identity.
- Clothing and environment may vary only when requested, and only as controlled variations grounded in the relevant original image.
- If a Studio feature cannot load the original asset, it must report the missing asset instead of silently rendering a substitute.
